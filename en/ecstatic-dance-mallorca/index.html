What is an ecstatic dance?

It is a space to dance freely, feel and connect. It is also called ecstatic dancing. It is not a class or a typical party: there is no choreography and no goal, just music, body and community.

A DJ guides the journey like a wave, from soft to intense and back to calm, and each person moves as they feel. We open with a circle and close with integration.

In Mallorca it is organized by FLOU: ecstatic dance in community, with music, nature and much more.

What a session with FLOU is like

  1. Opening circle. We arrive, look at each other and open the space together.
  2. Awakening the FLOU. A guided practice to wake up the body before dancing.
  3. Ecstatic dance. About two hours of free dancing with a DJ, barefoot and without talking.
  4. Integration and closing. A sound bath and a gentle ending to land.

  • No alcohol or drugs
  • No photos or videos
  • No judging
  • With respect and consent

Common questions

How long does an ecstatic dance last?

A dance session lasts about two hours. On 28 November 2026 FLOU is a full day, from 10:30 to 19:30, with two ecstatic dance sessions, cacao medicine, a sound bath and activities for kids.

How is it different from a nightclub?

There is no alcohol, nobody talks to you or takes photos, and you do not dance to impress anyone: you dance for yourself, barefoot and in silence, guided by a DJ's music.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move freely in. We dance barefoot. We provide the water and fruit.

Can I come alone?

Yes. It is a space without talking and without judgment, so you can come alone or with friends and dance your own way.

Where do I buy tickets?

Online tickets are on Brainpas, from €20. There are also tickets at the door.
